# Embryo Mesh VPN

Embryo Mesh VPN is Embryo-Zero's fork of [netbird](https://github.com/netbirdio/netbird) (332fb1c, commit 332fb1c). It does WireGuard mesh VPN with access control.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
